MachForge

«MachForge is an engineering-led CNC manufacturing coordination studio in Shenzhen, providing DFM review, CNC prototyping, and low-volume production support for robotics, UAV, and hardware teams.»

MachForge supports projects from engineering prototypes to low-volume production through vetted manufacturing partners in Shenzhen, China. We focus on DFM review, manufacturability decisions, supplier coordination, quality control, and reducing production risk before parts are cut.

Services

- CNC Milling & Turning
- DFM Review & Manufacturability Support
- CNC Prototyping
- Low-Volume Production
- Supplier Coordination & Production Follow-up
- Quality Control & Pre-Shipment Inspection
- Surface Finishing

Materials

- Aluminum: 6061, 7075
- Stainless Steel: 304, 316L
- Titanium: available on request
- Engineering Plastics

Manufacturing

- 3-axis, 4-axis, and 5-axis CNC machining
- CNC milling and turning
- Prototype quantities from 1 piece
- Low-volume production
- Surface treatments including anodizing, PVD, sandblasting, electropolishing, and laser marking

Industries

- Robotics
- UAVs
- Hardware Products
- Engineering Prototypes
- Low-Volume Hardware Production

Typical Use Cases

- Hardware startups needing CNC prototypes before production
- Robotics teams requiring precision metal components
- UAV companies coordinating low-volume production in China
- Engineering teams seeking DFM review before sending drawings to manufacturing partners
